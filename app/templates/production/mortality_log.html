Why do I need to record mortality?

Every hen that dies is one less hen laying eggs. Recording mortality keeps your flock size accurate, and your flock size is what drives your egg production and sales.

  • Fewer birds → fewer eggs → lower sales. If the flock drops a lot, your daily egg count and income will drop too.
  • Know when to add chickens. When the current flock falls well below the original, it is time to buy a new batch so production stays steady. Use Add Birds to record it.
  • Spot health problems early. Many deaths in a short time can mean disease or heat stress — call your veterinarian right away.
  • Correct cost per egg. Feed and expenses are shared by fewer birds, so tracking deaths shows your true cost and profit.
